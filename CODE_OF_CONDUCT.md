# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want EvoWrite to be an open, respectful, and constructive project for researchers, students, and developers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique text, rules, and behavior—not people.
- Respect privacy and do not post confidential manuscripts or data.
- Give proper attribution for ideas, examples, and derived work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Sharing private manuscripts, datasets, credentials, or unpublished results without permission.
- Plagiarism or knowingly misrepresenting another project's work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and constructive community.

Report concerns through a private channel or GitHub contact associated with the repository owner.
