# Third-Party Notices

EvoWrite contains original rules, examples, templates, and aggregate analyses created for Evolutionary Computation writing.

## Inspirations

- [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) — inspired the structural treatment of AI-writing tells, rewrite-before-final-check workflow, and the explicit distinction between human readability and AI-detector evasion.
- [Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing](https://github.com/Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing) — inspired the claim-forward, positive-scope, and calibrated-uncertainty treatment of defensive academic prose.

No source code, Skill file, or long text passage is copied from those projects. Patterns were redesigned and rewritten for Evolutionary Computation.
