Writing cases

Examples of the rewriting behavior EvoWrite is designed to produce. Examples are synthetic unless noted; no missing evidence is invented.

1. AI-sounding introduction

Before: With the rapid development of evolutionary computation, large-scale multiobjective optimization has become a hot topic. It is worth noting that our method may potentially significantly outperform state-of-the-art algorithms, although we do not claim universal optimality.

After: Large-scale multiobjective optimization remains challenging for evolutionary algorithms as the number of decision variables increases. On the tested problems, the proposed algorithm outperforms the state-of-the-art algorithms included in the comparison.

grand opening removedpositive scope

2. Unsupported performance claim

Before: The proposed robust and scalable algorithm significantly outperforms all state-of-the-art optimizers and proves that it is optimal.

Audit result: “prove/optimal” requires a formal guarantee; “all” requires an impossible comparison set; “significantly” requires a statistical protocol. Rewrite only after the relevant evidence is supplied.

claim calibrationaudit mode

3. Expensive optimization cost claim

Before: The surrogate-assisted algorithm is highly efficient because it converges in only 20 generations.

After: The surrogate-assisted algorithm reaches the reported solution quality within the stated budget of exact function evaluations; model-training overhead is reported separately.

FEs ≠ generationssurrogate cost

4. Defensive scope statement

Before: We do not claim that the method applies to all dynamic problems.

After: The evaluation covers dynamic problems with abrupt changes in the objective landscape.

5. Missing evidence during drafting

Input: expensive multiobjective optimization; Gaussian-process-assisted DE; goal is to reduce exact evaluations. No baselines, numbers, or results.

Expected behavior: draft only the problem, surrogate route, model-management issue, and DE motivation. Do not invent baselines, values, citations, or outcomes; list the missing mechanism and evaluation details after the draft.
