# Before/After: Conclusion

## Before

> Although this study still has some limitations, the proposed method may provide a useful reference for future research. In the future, we will further improve the algorithm and apply it to more complex real-world problems. We believe that the proposed method has broad application prospects and great potential.

## Problems

- 习惯性自我贬低；
- `useful reference`、`further improve`、`broad prospects` 空泛；
- 没有总结实际发现；
- 未来方向没有针对性。

## After

> This paper studied dynamic variable grouping for DE-based large-scale multiobjective optimization. Re-estimating groups during the search improved convergence on continuous LSMOP instances with interacting variables, while adding unnecessary overhead on weakly interacting problems. The current evaluation is limited to unconstrained continuous problems. Future work will examine overlapping groups and constrained LSMOPs, where the interaction structure is harder to estimate reliably.

## Why it reads more human

结论对应证据，边界只用一句说明，未来方向来自实际限制而不是宣传。
